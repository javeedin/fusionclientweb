This manual explains how to do everyday work in Re-ERP 3.2.6, one module per section, with the steps in the order you click them. It is written for finance, purchasing and administration users, not developers.

- **Screenshots** show the real screens filled with sample data (companies, suppliers and amounts are examples).
- **Menu paths** are written as *Module → Page*. You can also type a page name in **Search menu** at the top of every screen.
- **Business unit** matters on most pages: data, numbering, approvals and printed documents follow the business unit you choose.

| Module | What it covers | Main pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Ledger | Journals, chart of accounts, periods, trial balance, statements | Manage Journals, Create Journal, Trial Balance, Income Statement Templates |
| Accounts Payable | Suppliers, invoices, payments, accounting | Manage Invoices, Manage Payments, Suppliers, Create Accounting |
| Accounts Receivable | Customers, invoices, receipts, credit memos | Manage Receivable Invoices, Manage Customers, Manage Receipts |
| Cash Management | Bank transactions, statements, reconciliation | External Transactions, Bank Statements, Bank Reconciliation, Archived Statements |
| Fixed Assets | Asset register, depreciation, retirements | Manage Assets, Create Asset, Depreciation, Retirements |
| Purchasing | Requisitions, purchase orders, receiving, accruals | Requisitions, Purchase Orders, Receiving, Purchasing Approvals, Setup |
| Petty Cash, Rent, Portfolio | Petty cash registers, lease agreements, investments | Petty Cash Registers, Manage Agreements, Investment Holdings |
| Approvals | Approval rules and users, the notification bell | Administration → Approval Engine, the bell icon |
| Administration | Users, keys and tokens, FTP manager, AI tools | User Management, FTP Manager, AI Assistant |
