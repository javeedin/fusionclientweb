### Petty Cash

Petty Cash keeps a register for each cash float: its limit, cash account, owner, and the money paid in and out.

![Petty cash registers](other-01-petty-cash-registers.webp)

**Find a register**

1. Open **Petty Cash** from Home (or the *Petty Cash Registers* quick action).
2. Under *Search Criteria* filter by **Register Name**, **Business Unit**, **Status** (defaults to *Active*) and start date.
3. Click **Search**. Click a register name to open it in its own tab.

**Create a register**

1. Click **New Register**.
2. Enter the **Register Name** and **Business Unit** (required), then the currency, status (starts as *Draft*), start and end dates, **Owned By**, **Cash Limit**, **Cash Account** and comments.
3. Click **Save Register**.

Only *Draft* registers can be deleted.

### Rental Management

Rental Management holds the lease agreements for the company's properties, with their schedule of installments.

![Manage Rental Agreements](other-02-rm-agreements.webp)

1. Open **Rental Management → Manage Agreements** (or the *Manage RM Agreements* quick action).
2. Filter by **Status**, **Agreement #**, **Property** or **Tenant** and click **Search**. The list shows the property, tenant, start and end dates, number of months, payment frequency and total value.
3. Click the eye icon to view an agreement. A *DRAFT* agreement also has an **Activate** button.
4. To add one, click **New Agreement**, fill in the property, tenant, dates, rent and frequency, and click **Create Agreement**.

Statuses: *ACTIVE*, *DRAFT*, *EXPIRED*.

### Portfolio Management: Investment Holdings

**Portfolio Mgmt → Investment Holdings** shows the equity portfolio: book value, market value and unrealized profit or loss in USD, AED and INR, split by account type (NRE / NRO).

![Investment Holdings](other-03-pms-investment-holdings.webp)

- Filter by **Company**, **Share Type** and **Currency** (All, USD, AED, INR).
- Switch **View** between *Table* and *Cards*, and **Numbers** between abbreviated and full.
- Click **Refresh** to reload prices. Column headers sort the table.
