### Sign in

1. Open Re-ERP (the desktop app, or the web address your administrator gave you). The **Company** is filled in for you.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**. The app opens on Home.

![Sign-in page](start-01-login.webp)

Companies connected to Oracle Fusion also see **Login using Oracle Fusion** and a *Fusion Instance* picker.

### Set or reset your password

Use this the first time you sign in, or when you have forgotten your password.

1. On the sign-in page click **Reset / Set Password**.
2. Enter your e-mail address and click **Send OTP**. A 6-digit one-time password is e-mailed to you; it is valid for 15 minutes.
3. Enter the **OTP**, a **New Password** (at least 8 characters) and confirm it. Click **Set Password**.
4. Click **Go to Login** and sign in with the new password.

![Reset / Set Password](start-01b-reset-password.webp)

If the OTP does not arrive, click **Resend OTP**, or ask your administrator to reset the password for you in User Management.

### Home

![Home dashboard](start-02-home.webp)

- The greeting line shows today's date, the current GL period and whether it is open, and the app version.
- **KPI cards**: recent journals, GL period, AP invoices pending, and approvals waiting for you.
- **Modules**: one tile per module. Click a tile to open the module.
- **Quick Actions**: shortcuts to the screens used most often.
- **Recent Journals**: the latest journals, with **View all**.

### The top bar

The red bar stays at the top of every screen.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Re-ERP** logo and version | The app version is shown under the name, for example *3.2.6*. Quote it when you report a problem. |
| Company badge | The company you are signed in to. |
| **Search menu…** | Type part of a screen name, for example *invoice*, to jump to it (see below). |
| New window / new-tab toggle | Open another app window, or open search results in a new tab. |
| 🏠 Home | Back to Home. |
| 🔔 Notifications | Your notifications and approval requests. The red badge counts unread items. |
| My Tickets / Raise a Support Ticket | Your support tickets, or report a problem. |
| Training Library / Show & Tell | Training material and recorded demos. |
| **Errors** | Recent errors (GL validation log). |
| AI Assistant / Teach AI | Ask questions about your ERP data in plain language. |
| Avatar | **My Profile** and **Sign Out**. |

### Find a screen

Click **Search menu…** and type a few letters. Results are grouped by module; click one to open it.

![Searching the menu for "invoice"](start-03-menu-search.webp)

### Notifications and approval pop-ups

When someone sends you a document to approve, a yellow **Pending Approval** pop-up appears at the top right with **Approve**, **Reject** and **View**, and the bell badge goes up.

![Approval pop-ups after sign-in](start-05-approval-toast.webp)

Click the bell to open the Notifications panel. It opens on the **Approvals** filter; the other filters are *All*, *General* and one per module (GL, AP, AR, CM, RM, FA, PMS). Each row has **View details** (eye), **Approve** (green) and **Reject** (red).

![Notifications panel, Approvals filter](start-04-notifications.webp)

The panel refreshes every minute, and at once when a request is sent from your own browser. The full approval workflow is described in *Approvals & notifications*.

### Sign out

Click your avatar at the top right and choose **Sign Out**.
